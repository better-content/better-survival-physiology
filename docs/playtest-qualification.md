## Primary implemented gameplay promise

This mod integrates Diet's nutrient tracker with server-authoritative survival effects and temporary metabolic loads. Actual `SalienceEvents` applies nutrient-dependent mining, combat, movement, exhaustion, recovery and effect-duration behavior; `MetabolicState` carries sugar/Nectar, alcohol/Booze, blackout and cooldowns; `MetabolicStateStore` persists the state under `Player.PERSISTED_NBT_TAG/better_survival_physiology`, saves periodically and at logout, copies it on non-death clone and resets it on death. `DietBridge` uses the native Diet tracker and mirrors sugar/alcohol. `SalienceNetwork` and the client presentation classes display synced values; `DietScreenEvents` redirects Diet's screen opening to the inventory. These are source implementation claims, not a verified report of what build 69 players see. Inspect `runtime/SalienceEvents.java`, `nutrition/DietBridge.java`, `metabolism/MetabolicState*.java`, `client/`, `presentation/` and `network/` for the actual boundaries. Optional Spice/Water/Thirst/Brewing/Cold Sweat/Epic Fight behavior depends on compatible providers and installed pack binaries; do not infer typed Water support from the legacy Water runtime alone.

This mod registers **no acquireable gameplay item or block**: `SystemicSalienceMod` registers config, network and sounds, and the only `DeferredRegister` here is `presentation/ModSounds.java`. Acquisition for tested inputs belongs to other mods/vanilla: milk bucket and honey bottle (`minecraft`), milk bottle (`farmersdelight`), beer (`brewinandchewin`), apple juice and Curios water slot (`better_drinking_water`), chilli (`better_spicy_farmers`), and native Diet's group lookup. Lookup and thresholds are in `metabolism/ConsumableProfiles.java`, `config/SalienceConfig.java`, `src/main/resources/data/diet/diet/groups/`, `src/main/resources/data/diet/diet/suites/builtin.json`, and `src/main/resources/data/diet/tags/items/`; sugar tags live at `src/main/resources/data/better_survival_physiology/tags/items/`. The fixture's `item(id)` in `PhysiologyBeverageGameTests` fails on missing registered items; this is not proof of survival acquisition, recipes or loot accessibility. Recipe-root and real acquisition paths must be checked in the owning mods and full pack, not invented here. Source-defined native fixtures copy five Farmers Respite recipe/loot/advancement repair resources from `../../better-content-modpack/kubejs/data/farmersrespite/` into **test-only** resources; confirm the pack's actual runtime data separately.
